More

What can I type?
  • + - * / ^ and brackets. Put * between variables: m*v, not mv (that's one variable called mv).
  • sqrt(x), ln(x) or log(x) (natural log), log10(x), exp(x), abs(x)
  • sin cos tan, asin acos atan. Choose degrees or radians in the settings.
  • Constants: pi and e. Any other name is a variable, so E, c, phi are fine. Greek names like theta show as symbols, and v_max gives a subscript.
  • Numbers like 6.67e-11 or 6.67x10^-11. A constant you know exactly can be a variable with uncertainty 0.
  • Constants: name a variable g, c, h, hbar, k_B, N_A, R, G, q_e, epsilon_0, mu_0, m_e or m_p and a button offers to fill in its accepted value.
  • Repeated readings: press Repeats beside a variable to enter several readings and use their mean and standard error.
  • Units (optional), typed in each variable's unit box: m cm mm um nm, s ms min h, kg g, N J W Pa, V A ohm C F T, Hz, K degC, deg rad, and combinations like m/s^2 or kg m^2.
